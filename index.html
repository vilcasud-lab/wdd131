Arequipa, Peru

Arequipa is a wonderful city surrounded by volcanoes and has incredible cuisine! Arequipa, known as the "White City," is the second most populous and important city in Peru. Located in the south of the country, its historic center is a UNESCO World Heritage Site thanks to its impressive colonial architecture built from sillar (white volcanic stone) and its traditional picanterías (traditional restaurants).
