Overview

Purpose

The purpose of this website is to educate readers on milk kefir and traditional búlgaros de leche. It provides clear, step-by-step instructions on how to cultivate, care for, and ferment kefir grains, detailing their proven health benefits, probiotic properties, and delicious everyday recipes.

Scenarios

  • How do I properly feed and care for my milk kefir grains so they stay healthy and do not spoil?
  • What are the specific digestive and immune health benefits of drinking fermented milk kefir daily?
  • What simple recipes can I make using fruit, secondary fermentation, or strained kefir cheese?
